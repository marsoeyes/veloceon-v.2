    What this does
    1. Every candidate phrase is rebuilt from your known words and the checksum is verified (removes 93.75–99.6 % of combinations instantly).
    2. Surviving candidates are stretched with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 (2048 rounds) exactly like BIP39 requires.
    3. The seed is derived along the configured paths for every selected network and compared against your addresses.
    4. Nothing is sent anywhere: no RPC, no balance scanning, no telemetry.
